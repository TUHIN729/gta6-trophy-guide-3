    The road to the platinum

    About 200 hours, most of it after the story. Five stages, in order — and one save habit that protects the whole run.

    The one habit that matters

    GTA 6 has no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. That single habit is your insurance for the run's only missable trophy.

      What the checklist looks like at the end

      The 100% completion list (A Long Road) opens after the story and covers the counted trophies: 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 3 card sets, 18 meditation spots and 8 stranger strands. Earlier progress counts, so nothing on it is wasted effort.

      Roadmap built from the official trophy descriptions and the counts PlayStation tracks (PSNProfiles / Exophase). Times: about 200 hours to the platinum (Push Square, GTABase).

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy on the whole list is reported missable. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam.

      Why the rest are safe

      Every story trophy unlocks automatically by finishing its mission or chapter, and no story mission is one-shot — the danger is never the mission, it is a collectible tied to a chapter that ends. The side-character strands, activities, robberies and collectibles all stay available in free roam after the credits. The only reported exception is the meditation spot in Chapter 1, above.

      Missable call based on the official descriptions plus KeenGamer and Output Lag (November 2026). If later guides confirm another missable, this page will say so.

        How to use this guide

        1. Play the story first, ticking trophies as they pop, with a manual save at each chapter start.
        2. Do the meditation spots the moment you find them — Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable.
        3. After the credits, work the roadmap in order: side characters, activities, robberies, then collectibles and the 100% checklist.
        4. Never turn on a cheat before the platinum: cheats disable trophies (and saving) in GTA games.
        5. Your progress lives in this browser only. Export it before clearing browser data or switching devices.
